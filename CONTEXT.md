# World of Floorcraft

World of Floorcraft is a ballroom dance platform for syllabus reference, routine building, social collaboration, organizations, messaging, and competition operations.

## Language

### Syllabus

**Syllabus**:
The curated body of ballroom dance figures and allowed transitions used for technical reference and routine building.
_Avoid_: OCR data, graph data

**Dance**:
A ballroom dance whose figures are browsed together, such as Waltz, Tango, Viennese Waltz, Foxtrot, or Quickstep.
_Avoid_: Style when referring to syllabus dances

**Figure**:
A named syllabus movement within a dance, optionally with a variant, level, step chart, timing, and technique notes.
_Avoid_: Step, routine entry, pattern

**Figure Variant**:
A named alternate form of a figure that needs distinct identity when browsing or linking transitions.
_Avoid_: Treating variant text as only display decoration

**Syllabus Level**:
The teaching or examination grade attached to a figure or transition: Student Teacher, Associate, Licentiate, or Fellow.
_Avoid_: Competition level, medal level

**Transition**:
A directed allowed movement from one figure to another, optionally constrained by level or condition.
_Avoid_: Edge in product language

**Step Chart**:
The leader and follower technical breakdown of a figure's steps, including positions, alignment, turn, rise and fall, footwork, CBM, and sway.
_Avoid_: Raw extraction data

**Local Graph**:
A figure-centered view of the immediate transitions into and out of that figure.
_Avoid_: Neighbor graph in user-facing language

**Full Graph**:
A dance-level view of all figures and transitions for one dance.
_Avoid_: Whole graph

### Routines

**Routine**:
A named ballroom dance sequence assembled from syllabus figures for a specific dance.
_Avoid_: Sequence, plan, post

**Routine Entry**:
One occurrence of a figure at a specific place in a routine.
_Avoid_: Routine item, step

**Published Routine**:
A routine the creator has made visible on their profile.
_Avoid_: Routine share

**Routine Share**:
A social post that references a routine and presents it in the feed with post visibility and optional caption.
_Avoid_: Published routine, copied routine

### Social

**Profile**:
A dancer's public-facing identity in World of Floorcraft, including handle, display name, bio, competition level, and privacy setting.
_Avoid_: Account

**Private Account**:
A profile whose posts and routines are visible only to the owner and approved followers.
_Avoid_: Private profile, protected user

**Follow Request**:
A request to follow a private account; it becomes a follow only after approval.
_Avoid_: Pending follower

**Post**:
A feed item authored by a dancer or organization, either an article or a routine share.
_Avoid_: Note, entry

**Post Visibility**:
The audience rule for a post: public, followers, or organization.
_Avoid_: Privacy when referring to individual posts

**Following Feed**:
The signed-in feed of content visible through follows and organization membership.
_Avoid_: Home feed unless renamed intentionally

**Explore Feed**:
The discovery feed of recent public posts.
_Avoid_: Public feed if that would also include non-feed public pages

**All Saved**:
The default private saved-post collection for posts not placed in a named save folder.
_Avoid_: Default folder, unsorted folder

**Partner Search Profile**:
An opt-in listing that says a dancer is looking for a partner, with style, role preference, location, and short notes.
_Avoid_: Dating profile, competitor profile

**Competition Level**:
A dancer's self-declared competitive skill band, distinct from syllabus levels.
_Avoid_: Figure level, ISTD level, syllabus level

### Organizations

**Organization**:
A ballroom community entity, such as a studio, club, team, or organizing body, that people can join and that can publish content or host competitions.
_Avoid_: Account, team as a generic synonym

**Membership**:
The relationship between a user and an organization, granting participation in organization spaces and access to organization-only content.
_Avoid_: Follow, subscription

**Membership Model**:
The rule an organization uses for admitting new members: open, request, or invite.
_Avoid_: Role, privacy setting

**Owner**:
The person with final control of an organization, including ownership transfer and deletion authority.
_Avoid_: Admin

**Admin**:
A member delegated to manage organization settings, members, org posts, invites, and join requests, without being the owner.
_Avoid_: Owner

**Join Request**:
A pending request from a user to join a request-based organization, reviewed by organization admins.
_Avoid_: Invite, application

**Direct Invite**:
An invitation sent to a specific user to join an organization, which the invited user may accept or decline.
_Avoid_: Invite link

**Invite Link**:
A reusable organization invitation URL that lets anyone with the link accept membership before it expires.
_Avoid_: Direct invite

**Org Post**:
A post published on behalf of an organization rather than an individual user.
_Avoid_: Announcement unless it is specifically an announcement

**Org-Only Content**:
Content visible to members of a specific organization.
_Avoid_: Private, followers-only

**Host Organization**:
The organization under which a competition is created and managed.
_Avoid_: Organizer when referring to the organization rather than a person

### Messaging

**Conversation**:
A messaging thread that holds the shared history for a direct message, group chat, or org channel.
_Avoid_: Thread, chat, channel when referring to the general concept

**Direct Message**:
A private conversation between exactly two users.
_Avoid_: Private chat, one-to-one thread

**Group Chat**:
A conversation explicitly created for multiple selected people.
_Avoid_: Group conversation unless discussing the general model

**Org Channel**:
A named organization conversation available to members of that organization.
_Avoid_: Organization chat, team chat, group chat

**Conversation Member**:
A user with access to a conversation and per-user read or unread state for that conversation.
_Avoid_: Participant

**Message**:
A text entry sent by a conversation member into a conversation.
_Avoid_: Post, comment

**Presence**:
A temporary indication that a user is currently present in a specific conversation; it is not global online status.
_Avoid_: Online status

**Typing Indicator**:
A temporary signal that another conversation member is composing a message.
_Avoid_: Presence update

**Unread Count**:
The number of messages in a conversation that a member has not yet seen.
_Avoid_: Notification count

### Competitions

**Competition**:
A ballroom dance contest hosted by an organization, with a lifecycle from setup through published results and post-event follow-up.
_Avoid_: Event when referring to the whole contest

**Competition Status**:
The lifecycle state controlling visibility and available competition actions.
_Avoid_: Implementation phase

**Competition Event**:
The unit competitors enter and judges score: a style and level grouping containing one or more dances.
_Avoid_: Dance, heat, round

**Registration**:
A person's participation record for a competition, before or alongside the specific events they enter.
_Avoid_: Entry

**Entry**:
A leader and follower couple's participation in one competition event.
_Avoid_: Registration, dance entry

**Multi-Dance Event**:
A competition event containing multiple dances scored together for an overall placement.
_Avoid_: Grouped dance

**Scratch**:
A reversible operational withdrawal from an entry, distinct from deleting the entry.
_Avoid_: Drop, remove

**Add/Drop Request**:
A late request after entries close to add or drop an entry, reviewed by competition staff.
_Avoid_: Scratch

**TBA Listing**:
A partner-finder listing from a competitor seeking a leader or follower for a competition context.
_Avoid_: Team match, partner search profile

**Round**:
A stage of a competition event used to reduce or rank couples, such as preliminary rounds, semifinals, and finals.
_Avoid_: Heat

**Heat**:
A subdivision of a round containing a subset of couples dancing together.
_Avoid_: Round

**Callback**:
A preliminary-round mark indicating a judge wants a couple to advance.
_Avoid_: Placement

**Judge**:
A competition adjudicator in the global judge directory who does not need a platform account.
_Avoid_: Platform user

**Scrutineer**:
The official responsible for operating scoring, results, and day-of competition flow.
_Avoid_: Organizer when the role is specifically scoring authority

**Competitor Number**:
The number that identifies a couple on the floor, conventionally attached to the leader when that person leads.
_Avoid_: Personal number, per-person number

**Skating System**:
The ballroom scoring method used for final placements, including multi-dance tie handling.
_Avoid_: Scoring engine when naming the domain rule set

**Registration Check-In**:
Arrival, payment, and number check-in, once per person per competition.
_Avoid_: Deck captain check-in

**Deck Captain Check-In**:
Per-event floor-readiness status for a couple before dancing, operational and reversible.
_Avoid_: Registration check-in

### Design Language

**Metals**:
Bronze, silver, and gold as meaningful materials for syllabus levels, placements, earned status, or membership.
_Avoid_: Decorative metal accents
