## Anti-Patterns Verdict

This does not read like generic AI UI. The typography, square panels, monochrome base, and metals give it a distinctive syllabus-atellier identity. The main remaining risk is not generic taste but uneven application: some pages feel highly considered while sparse operational pages and inherited system surfaces still reveal framework defaults or unfinished composition.

## Overall Impression

The redesign has moved into a much stronger place. The public syllabus pages now feel intentional, restrained, and specific to ballroom reference material. The current weakness is hierarchy at scale: long prose, long figure names, relationship lists, and sparse pages need more editorial structure so the dramatic style remains useful rather than merely handsome.

## What's Working

- The brand identity is strong: Source Serif for editorial headings, SF-style body rhythm, JetBrains Mono metadata, square panels, and metallic tokens are cohering.
- The figure detail step table is the clearest product surface reviewed: dense, legible, and appropriate for repeated syllabus lookup.
- The recent pullback from bolder titles and heavy table rules was the right direction; the UI feels calmer and more premium without losing identity.

## Priority Issues

### P1: Long-form reference prose is too wide

The figure detail notes span a very wide measure. The detector also flagged line length around 149 characters. That slows reading and makes notes feel like a layout afterthought.

Fix direction: cap prose blocks around 65-75ch, or create a reference layout where long explanatory text gets a narrower column while metadata stays full-width.

Suggested skill: `/impeccable typeset` or `/impeccable layout`.

### P1: Sparse operational pages feel under-composed

The competitions page has a single card on the left with a CTA far to the right, leaving a lot of inactive space. The homepage also carries a large empty upper field before the main content resolves. Sparse data states need intentional composition, not just max-width emptiness.

Fix direction: tighten vertical rhythm, group page actions closer to the relevant content, and design empty or low-data states as explicit bands or rows.

Suggested skill: `/impeccable layout`.

### P2: Figure and relationship lists need stronger scan hierarchy

The Waltz figure list and the Preceded/Followed sections mix canonical names, variants, levels, and relationship notes in long inline runs. Power users can parse it, but it adds avoidable friction.

Fix direction: separate canonical title from variant notes, visually quiet parentheticals, group relationships by usefulness, and consider progressive disclosure for long relationship sets.

Suggested skill: `/impeccable clarify`.

### P2: Some inherited surfaces still bypass the design language

The detector found pure black overlay classes in dialog and sheet components, and the sign-in route still appears closer to Clerk/framework defaults. Dropdown blur has improved, but overlays and auth surfaces should use the same carbon/surface/rule tokens.

Fix direction: replace raw `bg-black` overlay usage with tokenized carbon overlay values, audit Clerk/auth styling, and remove any remaining layout-property transitions that survived the animation rollback.

Suggested skill: `/impeccable polish` or `/impeccable audit`.

### P3: Metal filter affordance could be clearer

Bronze, silver, and gold controls all read as filled material buttons. If they are multi-select filters, that is acceptable but should be unmistakable. If only one is selected, the current styling is ambiguous.

Fix direction: clarify selected versus available states without making the metals dull again.

Suggested skill: `/impeccable colorize`.

## Persona Red Flags

Dance teacher / syllabus power user: long variants and relationship lists slow fast lookup. Abbreviations are efficient but need quick recognition support.

Competition organizer: sparse competitions pages do not yet communicate operational confidence. The CTA and content should feel connected.

New dancer: abbreviations like CBM, SRSRSS, LOD, and TILT require domain knowledge with little inline help.

## Minor Observations

- The nav identity is strong; verify icon-only controls have accessible labels and visible focus states.
- Detector reported Inter overuse, but that appears mostly intentional or stale relative to the current font plan. Treat as low concern unless actual pages regress toward generic body-only typography.
- The pure black warning partly overlaps the approved carbon palette, but raw overlay classes should still be tokenized for consistency.
- The live Impeccable overlay command was unavailable in this CLI install, so this critique used rendered browser inspection plus `impeccable detect` instead.

## Questions to Consider

1. Should the next pass prioritize reading layout on figure details, sparse page composition, or token/overlay consistency?
2. For relationship lists, should the product optimize for complete graph visibility or fastest syllabus lookup?
3. Should the next audit include authenticated competition and communication pages, or stay focused on public pages first?
